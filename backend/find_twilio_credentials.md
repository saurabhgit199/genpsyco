# How to Find Your Twilio Account SID and Auth Token

## Steps:

1. **Go to Twilio Console**: https://console.twilio.com/

2. **Find Account SID and Auth Token**:
   - Look at the **dashboard homepage** - you'll see:
     - **Account SID**: Starts with "AC" (e.g., ACxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx)
     - **Auth Token**: Click "Show" to reveal it (starts with various letters)

3. **Alternative Method**:
   - Click on your **account name** (top right corner)
   - Go to **Account** → **API Keys & Tokens**
   - You'll see:
     - **Account SID**: Under "Account Info"
     - **Auth Token**: Under "Auth Token" (click "Show" to reveal)

4. **Important Notes**:
   - **Account SID** starts with "AC" (34 characters)
   - **Auth Token** is a long string (32 characters)
   - **Organization SID** (starts with "OR") is different and not used for API authentication

## What You Need:
- Account SID (starts with "AC")
- Auth Token (long string, click "Show" to see it)
- Phone Number: +14639460230 (you already have this)
